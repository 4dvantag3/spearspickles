What's in the Jar?

The Heavyweight

Classic Dill Spears

We could tell you these spears are crunchy. Lots of pickles say that. So instead, we'll tell you that during testing, one of our tasters bit into one and the person across the table asked if they'd stepped on a twig.

Fresh cucumbers, fresh dill, a truly unreasonable amount of garlic, and a cold brine that never sees a stove. That last part matters: cooking is what makes pickles soft, so we just… don't.

The Lightweight

Garlic Knockout Chips

Crinkle-cut so there's more surface area for brine to cling to. It's science. Delicious, garlicky science. Built for burgers, sandwiches and standing in front of the fridge.

Pairs well with: a grill and no plans.

The Southpaw

Hot Jab Spears

Our classic spear with a chili kick that doesn't show up right away. It waits. Then, about two chews in, it says hello.

Pairs well with: a cold drink. Trust us.

About Us

Pull up a chair.

Spears started on a back porch with one simple rule: a pickle should have some snap to it. No mushy spears. No sad, soggy chips.

We keep everything else easy. Real ingredients, packed cold, kept cold, straight to your fridge.
